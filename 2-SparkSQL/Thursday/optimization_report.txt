OPTIMIZATION REPORT
===================
Baseline Time:      8.17s
Optimized Time:     2.60s
Improvement:        68.2 % faster

Techniques Applied:
Caching: [Cached sales-customers and sales-products joins since they are used more than once within the pipeline]
Partitioning: [Reduced the number of shuffle partitions to 8, and repartitioned sales on 'customer_id' column to optimize its join with customers]
Bucketing: [Bucketed sales and customers on 'customer_id' and products on 'product_id' to reduce shuffles]

Key Learnings:
[My insight was that Spark performance is usually limited by data movement rather than computation. Optimizations like caching reused datasets and aligning
 partition strategies with join keys can drastically improve performance.]
[Just 5 joins and aggregations with a 50,000 row sales table already takes almost 10 seconds. This definitely shows that proper optimization is needed for big data.
 Caching and partitioning showed big improvements particularly]
